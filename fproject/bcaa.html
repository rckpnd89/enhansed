BCAAs

Apart from Protein Shakes, the next supplement that comes to mind when you are on a workout plan is a BCAA - and there is a good reason for that. The term BCAA means Branched Chain Amino Acids. BCAA contains 3 essential amino acids that help the synthesis of protein in your body. This simply means that healthier proteins in your muscle will replace or repair older proteins.The three amino acids in BCAA are: leucine, valine and isoleucine. At least one-third of your muscle tissue is made up of the three amino acids. They help your muscles get the proper nutrition, especially during workouts.
